## full log of changes and rationale

my main concerns were text formatting, fixing misalignments, having smooth/beautiful animation rather than all static, being much more consistent and aesthetically pleasing throughout.

### 1. splash/landing section ("TERAFAB")
- background video blurry and kind of ugly, the mass drivers video is incredible -- it's exciting and very aesthetically pleasing to open right off the bat.
- logo and call to action all much bigger and sharper.
- changed "watch now" button to "watch our launch" so it wouldn't seem like a TV show.
- little scroll arrow appears at the bottom after ~10secs of video has passed.
- changed vertical ordering of elements (partner logos underneath TERAFAB, then JOIN US).
- changed "JOIN US ON OUR JOURNEY" to "JOIN US" - latter is less cringe to me and cleaner.
- really didn't like the text formatting - drop shadows to me were ugly, also buttons and text shadows were ugly. made this much sharper.
- made the text appear on scroll and not just statically exist on the top page.
  
### 2. text and kardashev scale section
- made text appear on scroll instead of being static.
- changed all fonts to similar but cleaner one.
- boldened lines i thought should be emphasized.
- cleaner colors, instead of all white text with black drop shadows and ugly gray boxes.
- type1/2/3 boxes were really ugly to me! made them a sharper and visually pleasing timeline.
- made "THE KARDASHEV SCALE" appear in sequence and big/bold.
- still trying to fix the little pause in between kardashev video clips! in both original and my version. this should be smoother.

### 3. transition section
- added a "WHAT WILL IT TAKE?" transition. in the original, the transition from kardashev scale -> harnessing power of sun was quite abrupt, storywise, to me. wanted to reflect moving from theory to practice.

### 4. harnessing power of sun section
- nicer font

### 5. scaling to massive capacity section
